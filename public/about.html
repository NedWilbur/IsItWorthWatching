A method of questionable rigor

Less hype.
Better movie nights.

Sometimes a movie is more fun when nobody has spent six months telling you it will change your life. This little site helps you dodge the obvious duds without turning movie night into homework.

Search for a movie, pick the right one, and get a simple answer based on its TMDB audience rating. We call it a “yes” at 6 out of 10 or higher, once more than 20 people have weighed in. It’s a nudge, not a review. Your taste gets the final say.

Show the famously complicated algorithm

More than 20 ratings and an average of at least 6/10? Yes. Otherwise, not yet. Peer reviewed by exactly zero wizards.

Movie data and audience ratings are provided by The Movie Database (TMDB). This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
